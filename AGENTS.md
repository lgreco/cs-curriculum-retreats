# Repository agent guidance

This repository holds materials for the Department of Computer Science's
semiannual curriculum retreats at Loyola University Chicago. Coordinate work
across the repository here; keep retreat-specific decisions and materials in
the corresponding retreat directory.

## Retreat materials

- Keep each retreat in its own directory under `retreats/`.
- Preserve the existing directory name and structure when updating a retreat.
- Add an `AGENTS.md` to every retreat directory. Use it for that retreat's
  context and instructions; do not duplicate repository-wide guidance there.
- Do not assume facts, dates, decisions, or materials that are not present in
  the repository or supplied by the user.

## Changes

- Keep edits scoped to the retreat or shared material they affect.
- Preserve the original meaning of meeting notes and other source materials.
- Follow existing formatting and organization, and avoid adding tooling unless
  requested or necessary.
