# Fall 2026 Curriculum Retreat

## Readings

* [Paralysis](https://proofsandprompts.com/2026/10/21/artificial-intelligence-and-the-mathematical-community/): Sakellaridis, a Johns Hopkins mathematician, argues that AI companies’ push to recoup massive investment drives them to capture and own scientific output: they train on publicly funded research without attribution, operate opaquely, and threaten to displace human mathematical understanding. He faults professional organizations, universities, prominent mathematicians, and policymakers for a collective “paralysis” that leaves this unchecked and may push a generation of aspiring scientists away from mathematics. This is relevant because his argument is extensible to other STEM fields, including computing.
